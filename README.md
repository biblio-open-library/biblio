# BIBLIO
### Stories Worth Knowing.

Biblio is a free, independent library of compelling true stories, knowledge and human experiences from around the world.

Our mission is to make fascinating, meaningful and well-sourced stories accessible to everyone.

## Our Principles

- 100% free to use.
- No advertising.
- Optional voluntary donations.
- Open community story submissions.
- Credible sources and factual accuracy.
- Human editorial oversight.
- AI-assisted research and content support, with accuracy as a priority.

## The Community

Biblio is built around its readers and contributors.

Users can:
- Discover extraordinary true stories.
- Submit their own stories.
- Share knowledge and experiences.
- Rate stories from 1 to 100.
- Help maintain quality through community participation.

## Our Mission

To create a beautiful, trustworthy and accessible home for the world's most fascinating true stories.

Knowledge should be shared, not hidden behind a paywall.
